# Word Scramble — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Unscramble jumbled letter tiles into hidden words. Tap the tiles or just
type, grab hints, and chase combos in 60-second Blitz — or unwind in Zen
mode. 1,100+ words, one tiny free file.

## Details

- Genre: Word / Puzzle / Casual
- Controls: Mouse / Touch (tap letter tiles, tap an answer slot to take a
  tile back). Keyboard: A–Z to type, Backspace to undo, Space to shuffle.
- Features: 1,117 common 5–7 letter words in 19 categories, anagram-deduped
  bank (every scramble has exactly one valid answer), 60-second Blitz with
  combo streaks, 5-word Zen mode, Hint (−20, max 2 per word) and Skip
  (−30), time & combo bonuses, wood/neon themes, best scores and longest
  streak saved locally, synthesized WebAudio sound with mute, single-file
  HTML5 with no external assets, works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, DOM + CSS animations, WebAudio. No frameworks,
  no tracking, no external links.
